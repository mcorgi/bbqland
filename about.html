Our Story

Our story

Every dish at BBQ Land begins with premium ingredients and exquisite plating.

From sirloin steak and short rib sizzling on the grill, to carefully crafted Asian dishes from our kitchen, each is thoughtfully developed and refined, reflecting our passion for composing a symphonic balance of modern Asian barbecue. And our team is more than your servers: they're your guides at the grill, ready with a tip or a helping hand whenever you need it, while giving you the space to enjoy both the cooking and the company around the table.

Brought to you by Z&N Restaurant Group, creators of Hao Shi Guang and Kis.O, BBQ Land builds on more than a decade of barbecue expertise. It is our way of bringing the cross-Pacific traditions of timeless hospitality, quality ingredients, and cherished culinary craft to Shrewsbury. Whether you're celebrating with family or gathering with friends, we invite you to experience Asian barbecue as a blending of cultures, where every meal is cooked and shared right at your table.

Our Philosophy

Three things we never compromise.

01

Premium ingredients

Our meat comes from Savenor's, the Cambridge butcher Julia Child trusted for decades, from A5 wagyu to Berkshire pork.

02

Tableside grilling

Every table has its own brass-ringed grill. You set the pace: sear the wagyu for seconds, crisp the pork belly, take your time.

03

Heartfelt hospitality

Every plate is crafted to bring people together. We're close by with a tip, a fresh plate, or a refill whenever you need one.

Our Standard

At a grill table, nothing hides the meat.

Yakiniku is simple on purpose: a hot grill, a quick sear, and a dip of sauce. There's no braise or heavy gravy to cover for an average cut.

So we're relentless about what reaches your table, and about running a restaurant that lives up to it. It's why our beef comes from Savenor's, the historic Cambridge butcher famously known as Julia Child's butcher.

01

Marbling you can see

From Japanese A5 wagyu to prime kalbi, the fat is the flavor. Watch it melt on the grate.

02

Seconds, not hours

Thin slices cook in moments at your table, so every cut has to be good enough to eat almost as-is.

03

A partner we trust

Working with a butcher with eighty-plus years of standards behind it lets us focus on the grill and the hospitality.
