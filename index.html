Our Standard

Relentless about every detail.

We've spent more than a decade running restaurants, and we know a great night comes from getting the small things right, every time: the cut, the fire, the timing, the refill before you ask.

That standard is why our beef comes from Savenor's, the historic Cambridge butcher famously known as Julia Child's butcher. At a grill table the meat is the meal, and theirs is worth the fire.
